Everything happens on your device

A redaction app that sends your documents to a server would defeat its own purpose. This one does not have a server to send them to.

  • Detection runs on the device. Faces and text are found with Apple’s Vision framework, on your iPhone.
  • Speech is transcribed on the device. On-device recognition is required in code; the audio is never uploaded.
  • No account, no login, no server. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
  • No analytics, no third-party SDKs. Nothing measures what you open or what you redact.
  • Redactions are burned into the pixels. On export the hidden areas are redrawn as new pixels, so the original detail is not inside the exported file.
  • Exports carry no metadata. No EXIF, no GPS coordinates, no device information travels with the file you share.

One honest exception: if you choose Replace Original, the app asks Photos to replace the asset in your own library, and Photos keeps that asset’s original capture date and location on the record. Export a new copy instead if you would rather not keep them.
