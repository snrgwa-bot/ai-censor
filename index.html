Hide what shouldn't be shared

Auto Redact covers faces, names, addresses and account numbers in photos and PDF documents before you send them. Everything happens on your iPhone — nothing is uploaded, and there is no account.

Three ways to hide something

Drag a box, rounded rectangle or oval over anything. Tap individual words. Or let the app find faces and sensitive details for you.

It finds what you'd miss

Names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, card and account numbers, national IDs, passports, passwords, dates of birth, licence plates, web and IP addresses.

You decide what actually goes

A review sheet lists everything found, by category. Switch a whole category off, or keep one specific email visible while the rest is covered.

Four ways to cover

Black bar, mosaic, blur, or an emoji sticker on an opaque plate. Adjust the strength, and change your mind at any point — the style applies to everything on the page.

Photos and documents

Up to 20 photos at once, or a PDF up to 50 pages. Redact page by page, or apply the automatic tools across the whole batch.

Check before you send

Pinch to zoom in up to 10× to confirm everything really is covered. One finger still draws, two fingers pan.

Why the redactions are real

A black rectangle drawn on top of a photo hides nothing — the original pixels are still in the file, and the shape can be peeled away. Auto Redact does not work that way.

When you export, the image is composited again from scratch and the covered areas are replaced with new pixels. The hidden detail is not stored anywhere in the exported file and cannot be recovered from it. The file also carries no EXIF, no GPS coordinates and no device information, because it is encoded from a bare bitmap.

PDF pages are turned into images before being written back out, so the exported document contains no text layer at all. This matters: the most common redaction failure is a black box drawn over text that is still selectable underneath. There is nothing underneath here.

An honest note about blur

A light blur can sometimes be partially reversed by software. For identity documents, licence plates and anything you would not want recovered, use a black bar or a strong mosaic. The app tells you this at the moment it matters.
