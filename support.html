Using the app

How do I cover something by hand?

Choose Draw, pick a shape and a style, then drag across the part you want hidden. Choose Erase and tap a redaction to remove it.

How do I hide just one word?

Choose Words. The app reads the page and outlines every word. Tap one to hide it, tap it again to bring it back. Words that look sensitive are outlined in red.

How do I check that everything is covered?

Pinch to zoom in — up to ten times. One finger still draws, two fingers move the image around, and a small chip in the corner takes you back to the full view.

Can I change the style after redacting?

Yes. Picking a different style, shape or strength updates everything already covered on the page, not just the next thing you draw.

Can I work on several photos at once?

Yes — up to 20. They open as a multi-page document, and a control at the top of the tool panel lets you apply the automatic tools to just the photo you are looking at, or to all of them.

Why did "Cover Document" not find anything?

That tool looks for a card, receipt or page inside a photo — an ID held in a hand, for example. If your photo is already a full page, there is no separate document to find. Use Cover All Text, or draw over the parts you want hidden.

Something sensitive was missed.

Automatic detection reads machine-printed text. Handwriting, very low-quality scans and unusual layouts can defeat it. Always check the result yourself before sending — the automatic tools are there to save you time, not to replace looking.

Privacy and safety

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. All detection and redaction happen on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

Can someone undo a redaction in the file I sent?

No. The exported file is composited again from scratch, so the hidden detail is not present in it. The export also carries no EXIF, GPS or device information. PDF exports contain no text layer, so nothing is selectable underneath a black bar.

Is blur safe?

A light blur can sometimes be partially reversed by software. For identity documents, licence plates and anything you would not want recovered, use a black bar or a strong mosaic.

Does "Replace Original" delete my photo?

No. It edits the photo in your library, and Photos keeps the edit revertible. Note that Photos stores capture date and location on the photo's own record, so replacing the image data removes the file's EXIF but the library still knows where it was taken. Saving as a new photo or sharing gives you a completely clean file.
