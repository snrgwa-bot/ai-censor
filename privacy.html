Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

AI Censor does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. This policy is short because there is very little to describe.

What we collect

Nothing. AI Censor has no backend of any kind. We cannot see your photos, your documents, or anything you do in the app, because none of it is ever transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Your photos and documents

Photos and PDFs you open are processed entirely on your iPhone. Face detection and text recognition use Apple's Vision and Natural Language frameworks, which run on the device. Nothing is uploaded for processing.

The app keeps no library and no history. A document you open exists only while you are working on it, and is discarded when you close it.

Photo library access

When you save a redacted image, iOS asks your permission to add to your photo library. AI Censor requests add-only access for this — it does not read your library. If you choose "Replace Original", the app asks for read and write access, because editing a photo in place requires it. That edit is revertible from the Photos app.

Camera access

If you take a photo inside the app, the camera is used only to capture that image, which stays on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app asks Apple whether a purchase is active; that is the only thing it checks.

Network use

The app makes one kind of network request: asking Apple's public App Store lookup endpoint whether a newer version of AI Censor has been released, so it can offer you an update. This request contains only the app's own identifier. It never includes anything about you or your files, and if it fails the app simply carries on.

Children

AI Censor is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Everything the app stores lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
