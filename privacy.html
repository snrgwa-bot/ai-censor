Privacy Policy

AI Censor and Redactor · Essential Dev Team · Last updated 10 September 2026

The short version. AI Censor and Redactor does not collect anything. There is no account, no login, no server of ours, no analytics and no third-party SDK. Your photos, documents and recordings are processed on your device and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We could not look at your files even if we wanted to, because they never reach us.

1. Who we are

AI Censor and Redactor (“AI Censor”, “the app”) is an iPhone app published by Essential Dev Team (Dumitru Bumbu). This policy explains how the app handles your information. It applies to the app itself and to this website.

2. What we collect

Nothing. To be specific, the app does not collect, transmit, store on our systems, sell, rent or share any of the following:

  • Your photos, PDFs, audio recordings or any file you open in the app.
  • The contents of any redaction — the names, numbers, addresses or faces you cover.
  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • Your location.
  • Contacts, calendars, health data or messages.
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers or an IDFA.
  • Usage analytics, event logs, crash telemetry sent to us, or behavioural profiles.

There is no account system: you cannot sign up, you cannot sign in, and there is no user record to create, export or delete. There is no backend server operated by us that the app talks to. There is no analytics SDK and no third-party SDK embedded in the app — no advertising network, no attribution kit, no crash reporter of ours.

This website is a set of static pages served by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. GitHub, as the host, may process standard server request information (such as IP address) for the purpose of serving the pages and protecting the service; we do not receive that information.

3. On-device processing

All of the app’s work happens on your iPhone.

  • Face and text detection uses Apple’s Vision framework, which runs locally on the device. Detected faces, words, phone numbers, email addresses, card numbers, addresses and ID numbers are held in memory only for as long as you have the file open.
  • Speech transcription, used to let you bleep out spoken words, is set to require on-device recognition in the app’s code (requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true). Your audio is not uploaded to us, and is not sent to Apple’s servers for transcription. The transcript exists on your device only.
  • Redaction and export — blurring, mosaicking, black bars, stickers, erasing, watermarking, PDF flattening and audio bleeping — are performed on the device.

The app does not require an internet connection to do any of this.

4. Permissions the app may ask for

Each permission is requested only when you use the feature that needs it, and each can be withdrawn at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security on your iPhone. Declining a permission disables that one feature and nothing else.

  • Photos. To let you open a photo to redact, and to save the redacted result back to your library. Photos you open are read on the device and are not uploaded.
  • Camera. To let you take a photo inside the app and redact it immediately. The app does not record video and does not use the camera in the background.
  • Microphone. To let you record audio inside the app for bleeping. Recordings stay on the device.
  • Speech Recognition. To transcribe audio so you can choose which spoken words to bleep. The app requires on-device recognition, so the audio is not sent off the device for transcription.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, calendars, reminders, health data or motion data.

5. Exported files and metadata

When you export, the app draws a new file. The covered areas are redrawn as new pixels, so the original detail underneath a redaction is not present in the exported file — it is not merely hidden behind a shape that could be removed later. Exported PDFs are flattened for the same reason.

Exported files carry no EXIF metadata, no GPS coordinates and no device information. Where the file goes afterwards is entirely your choice, through the standard iOS share sheet. Once you share a file with another app or service, that app or service’s own privacy policy applies to it.

6. The “Replace Original” option

We want to be precise about one exception. If you choose Replace Original, the app asks the Photos app to replace that asset in your own photo library with the redacted version. Photos handles this itself and asks you to confirm it.

Because Photos is updating an existing library item rather than creating a new one, Photos keeps that item’s own capture date and location on the record, even though the redacted image file itself contains no EXIF or GPS data. This information stays inside your library on your device (and in your iCloud Photos library, if you use it) — it is not sent to us. If you would rather not keep it, export a new copy instead and delete the original yourself.

7. Purchases and Apple’s role

AI Censor Pro is sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system using StoreKit. All payment processing is performed by Apple. The app never sees, receives or stores your payment details — no card number, no billing address, no Apple ID password.

Your entitlement is checked against your Apple ID by the operating system, which is why restoring a purchase on a new device works without any login of ours. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated sales and subscription reports (for example, how many units were sold in a country); these contain no information that identifies you. Apple’s handling of your purchase is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

8. Sharing and third parties

We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not have any of your information to share. We do not sell or rent data, and we do not use your files to train any model.

The only third party involved in the app is Apple, in its role as the App Store and payment processor, and as the provider of the operating-system frameworks (Vision, Speech, Photos, StoreKit) that run on your device.

9. Storage and retention

Files you open and files you export are stored on your device, under your control. Temporary working files created during editing live in the app’s own sandbox and are removed when they are no longer needed; deleting the app removes them along with the app’s settings. Your preferences (such as your default redaction style) are stored locally on your device.

We hold no copies of anything, so there is nothing for us to retain or to delete on request. If you email us for support, we keep that correspondence only for as long as it takes to help you.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Since the app collects no personal information at all, there is no children’s data for us to hold. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information — for example by emailing us — contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete and port the personal data a company holds about you, and to object to its sale. We hold no personal data about you, so in practice there is nothing to access, correct, port or delete, and we have never sold or shared personal information. You retain full control of your own files on your own device at all times. If you have a question about this, write to us and we will answer it.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes for the version that introduces them. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy. Previous versions are available in this site’s public repository history.
